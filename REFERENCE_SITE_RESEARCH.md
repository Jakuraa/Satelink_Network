# REFERENCE SITE RESEARCH REPORT
## Deep Analysis of Claude.com and Anthropic.com for Information Architecture, Positioning, Trust & Discoverability

**Date:** 2026-10-16  
**Auditor:** Senior Website Auditor & Information Architect  
**Targets Analyzed:**  
- `https://claude.com/` (Flagship Product Experience)  
- `https://www.anthropic.com/` (Parent Corporate & Safety Research Institution)  
**Execution Context:** Evidence-led repositioning for Satelink Network (`https://satelink.network/`) and Jakuraa (`https://jakuraa.com/`).

---

## 1. Executive Summary

A thorough inspection of `https://claude.com/` and `https://www.anthropic.com/` reveals a clear, complementary separation of concerns between **Product Flagship** and **Corporate/Research Institution**:

1. **Anthropic.com (The Institution / Parent Entity):**
   - **Identity:** "Anthropic is an AI safety and research company that's working to build reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems."
   - **Role:** Explains *why* the institution exists, its research teams (Alignment, Interpretability, Frontier Red Team, Economics, Societal Impacts), governance commitments (Claude's Constitution, Responsible Scaling Policy, Transparency), institutional leadership, and company disclosures.
   - **Relationship to Product:** Points directly to Claude as its flagship technology product ("Try Claude", "Claude.ai", "Claude Console", "About Claude"), but never pretends that Anthropic *is* Claude.
   
2. **Claude.com (The Flagship Product Platform):**
   - **Identity:** "Claude is a next generation AI assistant built by Anthropic and trained to be safe, accurate, and secure to help you do your best work."
   - **Role:** Delivers high information density across audience segments (Developers, Enterprise, Individuals), clear product taxonomy (Core Claude, Claude Code, Specialized tools, Platform/API, Model families), capabilities (Artifacts, Connectors, Skills), and use cases (AI Agents, Coding, Commerce).
   - **Relationship to Parent:** Explicitly attributes ownership and research backing to Anthropic PBC via structured data, header links, and footer governance disclosures.

This parent-product duality is the exact blueprint required for **Jakuraa Commercial Private Limited** (the corporate technology institution) and **Satelink** (the flagship AI-agent and machine-commerce infrastructure platform).

## 2. In-Depth Analysis across 10 Strategic Dimensions

### Dimension 1: Positioning
- **Anthropic.com:** First screen leads with institutional mission, research rigor, and frontier AI safety. High-credibility, understated tone, serif/sans typographic balance.
- **Claude.com:** First screen explains immediate utility and platform capability. Answer-first definition: what it is, who built it, and what problem it solves.
- **Application to Satelink & Jakuraa:**
  - *Jakuraa:* Must lead with corporate technology institution identity: "Building software infrastructure for an agent-driven economy." Explains engineering focus, statutory basis, and Satelink as flagship platform.
  - *Satelink:* Must lead with platform-level machine-commerce infrastructure: "Infrastructure for AI agents that discover and use digital services." Not an "AI trading operating system" monopoly.

### Dimension 2: Audience Segmentation
- **Anthropic.com:** Institutional partners, researchers, policymakers, enterprise buyers, prospective talent.
- **Claude.com:** 
  - *Developers:* "Build with Claude Code and Claude Platform" (APIs, SDKs, Console, Docs, Academy).
  - *Enterprise:* "Overview, Security, Enterprise controls, Departmental use cases".
  - *End Users & Teams:* Direct access to web and desktop applications.
- **Application:**
  - *Satelink:* Segments into Autonomous AI Agents (machine buyers), Developers & API Providers (machine sellers / infrastructure users), and Enterprise (budget controls, usage attribution, dedicated keys).

### Dimension 3: Navigation Architecture
- **Anthropic.com Navigation:**
  - Research (Overview, Alignment, Economics, Engineering, Frontier Red Team, Interpretability, Science, Societal Impacts)
  - Policy & Commitments (Constitution, RSP, Transparency, Trust Center)
  - Learn (Academy, Tutorials, Use Cases, Developer Docs)
  - Company (About, Leadership, Careers, Events)
  - News
  - Primary Action: "Try Claude" (external product link)
- **Claude.com Navigation:**
  - Products (Claude, Claude Code, @Claude; Specialized; Capabilities; Models)
  - Developers (Build with, For developers, Documentation, Console)
  - Enterprise (Overview, Use Cases, Industries)
  - Pricing
  - Actions: Log in, Download app, Contact sales
- **Application:**
  - *Jakuraa:* Header links: Systems, Research, Technology, History, Company, Contact + external link to Satelink.
  - *Satelink:* Products (Machine Commerce, API Metering, Satelink Trading AI [Beta]), Solutions (AI Agents, Commerce, Trading Systems, Developers, Enterprise), Pricing, Developers (Docs, Quickstart, RPC, x402, MCP), Resources (Blog, News, Academy).

### Dimension 4: Information Density
- **Reference Standard:** Neither Anthropic nor Claude uses vacuous marketing slogans. Every heading is supported by concrete specifications, pricing tables, code blocks, parameter definitions, or verifiable research citations.
- **Application:** Both Satelink and Jakuraa must replace vague superlatives with exact endpoint URLs, flat-rate pricing ($0.00003/call), verified smart contract addresses (`RevenueVaultV2` on Polygon 137), exact HTTP 402 handshake specs, and explicit capability boundaries.

### Dimension 5: Content Progression
- **Observed Flow:** Problem Definition → Product Categorization → Architecture/Workflow → Verifiable Evidence/Code → Capability Boundaries & Disclaimers → Concrete Next Step (CTA).
- **Application:** 
  - Satelink homepage flow: Positioning Hero → Programmatic Workflow Loop (Discover, Evaluate, Authorize, Meter, Pay, Settle) → Control Gates (GO, WAIT, REJECT) → Safety Boundary (AI Proposes, Deterministic Limits Decide) → Interfaces (Web, API, MCP) → Active Services (Machine Commerce, RPC, x402) → Clear FAQs → Structured Beta Request.

### Dimension 6: Product Taxonomy
- **Reference Pattern:** Clear separation between:
  1. *Platform / Rails:* Claude Platform / API.
  2. *Flagship Products:* Claude desktop/web.
  3. *Specialized Applications:* Claude Code, Claude Security.
  4. *Models / Engines:* Opus, Sonnet, Haiku.
- **Application for Satelink:**
  1. *Core Platform:* Satelink Machine Commerce (programmatic discovery, metering, settlement).
  2. *Payment & Protocol Rails:* x402 (Base USDC HTTP 402), RevenueVaultV2 (Polygon USDT credit deposits).
  3. *Infrastructure Services:* Polygon PoS RPC Gateway.
  4. *Flagship Applications:* Satelink Trading AI (first application, private beta / paper trading only), Trading Intelligence API (metered research analytics).

### Dimension 7: Trust, Safety & Disclaimers
- **Reference Pattern:** Anthropic places safety, policy, and evaluation limitations prominently. Disclaimers are not hidden in micro-text; they are part of the core product explanation (e.g. system cards, acceptable use policies, safety evaluations).
- **Application:**
  - Satelink Trading AI must prominently display: "Paper trading in controlled beta. Live execution is not yet available. AI proposes; deterministic risk and your signed mandate decide. Satelink never takes custody of customer funds; withdrawals are never enabled."
  - Non-advice disclaimer must be visible on every financial data/trading surface.
  - Node operator program must state: "The node operator program is paused. Operator payouts are not active."

### Dimension 8: Conversion Matching Intent
- **Reference Pattern:**
  - Mature live tools: Direct self-serve action ("Try Claude", "Developer Quickstart").
  - Enterprise/Volume: "Contact sales".
  - Specialized/Experimental tools: Waitlist / Controlled preview.
- **Application:**
  - Live RPC & Discovery: Direct unauthenticated curl commands and quickstart links.
  - Trading AI: "Request beta access" (points to `/contact-sales?interest=beta`). Never "Trade now" or "Sign up to trade".

### Dimension 9: Search & Technical SEO Foundations
- **Reference Pattern:**
  - Distinct title formats: `<Page Name> | <Brand>`.
  - Canonical URLs pointing to single source of truth for each concept.
  - Semantic heading hierarchy: single `H1`, descriptive `H2` and `H3`.
  - Rich JSON-LD schemas: `WebSite`, `Organization`, `Brand`, `SoftwareApplication`.
- **Application:** Apply strict JSON-LD schemas and canonical discipline across both sites. Remove conflicting metadata.

### Dimension 10: GEO / AI Discoverability
- **Reference Pattern:** Pages begin with clear, standalone entity definitions (answer-first definitions under 50 words). AI crawlers can extract exact quotes explaining what the organization is without ambiguity.
- **Application:** Every product and company page contains an explicit definition block:
  - "Satelink is AI-agent and machine-commerce infrastructure..."
  - "Jakuraa Commercial Private Limited is a corporate technology institution..."

## 4. Synthesis for Implementation

1. **Jakuraa (`apps/corporate`):** Adopt the Anthropic institutional pattern. Anchor the site around Jakuraa Commercial Private Limited as the parent corporate technology institution founded in 2019 in Coimbatore, India. Feature Satelink as its flagship technology platform.
2. **Satelink (`apps/web`):** Adopt the Claude platform pattern. Anchor the site around AI-agent and machine-commerce infrastructure (discovering, pricing, metering, and settling API services). Re-integrate Satelink Trading AI as the first major application (controlled private beta), and metered RPC as an existing active service.
3. **Execution Guardrails:** Keep all existing UI components, fonts (Manrope, IBM Plex Mono, serif), color palettes (ivory, ink, green, stone), button designs, and layouts 100% intact. Surgical copy and metadata changes only.
